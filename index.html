I study safe learning and control for robotic systems. My work brings together Hamilton–Jacobi reachability, learning-based control, and trustworthy AI.

About

Research with safety in mind.

I am a PhD student in Computing Science at Simon Fraser University and a member of the Multi-Agent Robotic Systems Lab. I am interested in the boundary between formal safety guarantees and learning-based methods: how can autonomous systems act capably while respecting the limits that keep people and machines safe?
